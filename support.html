Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no account and we have no server that holds your streaks, logs or notes. If you use iCloud, the App can sync them between your own devices through your private iCloud space.

Will I lose my streaks if I delete the app or get a new phone?

Deleting the app removes its local data. If iCloud is on, your data can come back when you reinstall the App while signed in to the same Apple ID. To be safe, keep iCloud enabled before switching devices.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account: iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Quitter. Deleting the App does not cancel it.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall or Settings in the App and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID used to buy Pro.

How do I turn on Content Shield?

Open the Content Shield screen in the App and follow the steps, then enable it in iOS Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Content Blockers.

What do I do after a relapse?

Log it in the App, name the trigger and make a plan for next time. You keep your clean days. A relapse is a setback, not the end of the road.

I need urgent help.

Quitter is not a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.

Still need help?
